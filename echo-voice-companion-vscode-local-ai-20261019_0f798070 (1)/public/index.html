How do I add more voices?

Echo can use voices provided by your browser or operating system; it cannot install or create them. After installing a voice, return here and choose Refresh voices. Your browser decides which installed voices it exposes.

  • Windows: Open Narrator settings with Win + Ctrl + N, choose Add legacy voices, then in Speech settings use Manage voices → Add voices. See Microsoft's voice instructions.
  • macOS: Open System Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak, open the voice menu, and choose Manage Voices. See Apple's guide.
  • Android: Open Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output → Install voice data, then choose a language. See Google's instructions.

In CodePen, voice availability and microphone permissions are controlled by the browser/device, not by CodePen. Choose the sample-phrase language in the header; a profile's preferred voice is used for matching languages, with a closest-language fallback for others.
